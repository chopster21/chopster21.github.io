What Chopster is

A publishing tool for creators, not a marketing platform

Chopster is installed and run by the creator on their own device. It exists to remove the repetitive manual steps between finishing a video and posting it, while leaving every decision in the creator's hands.

Prepare

The creator selects their own video file and writes their own caption, title and privacy choice inside the app.

Publish

Chopster sends that video to the creator's own TikTok account using the official TikTok Content Posting API — never to anyone else's account.

Confirm

The creator chooses whether uploads arrive as a draft in their own TikTok inbox for review, or as a direct post they have authorised.

How it works

Four steps, all started by the creator

Nothing happens automatically or on someone else's behalf. Every publish action begins with the account owner.

  1. Install and run ChopsterIt runs on the creator's own Windows computer. No Chopster account is required to install it.
  2. Connect a TikTok accountThe creator signs in on TikTok's own website and approves the specific permissions Chopster requests. Chopster never sees or stores the password.
  3. Select a video and confirm the detailsThe creator picks their own file, writes their own caption, and chooses the privacy setting.
  4. Chopster delivers it to TikTokThe upload is sent to that creator's own account. In draft mode, the creator still presses Post themselves inside the TikTok app.

Your data

Everything stays on the creator's own device

Chopster operates no server and runs no cloud service, so there is nowhere for us to collect your data even if we wanted to.

Locally stored only

Access tokens, settings and job history are written to the user's own Windows profile. They are never transmitted to us.

Nothing is sold or shared

No data is sold, rented, shared with advertisers, or used to train anything. There is no analytics or telemetry in the app.

Revocable at any time

Disconnecting the account inside Chopster deletes the stored tokens immediately, and access can also be revoked from TikTok's own settings.

Scope of the integration

Which TikTok features Chopster uses, and why

Login Kit

Used so the creator can authorise Chopster to act on their own account. Only user.info.basic is requested, and solely to show which account is connected.

Content Posting API — video.upload

Used to deliver the creator's own video into their own TikTok inbox as a draft. The creator reviews it and taps Post inside the TikTok app. Chopster does not publish anything without the creator's confirmation.
