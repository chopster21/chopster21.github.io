Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information the Chopster desktop application processes, why it processes it, where that information is stored, and what control you have over it.

Last updated: 15 October 2026

The short version

Chopster runs entirely on your own computer. We operate no server and no cloud service, so we never receive, collect, or hold your content, your tokens, or your personal data. Everything the app needs stays on your device, and you can delete it at any time.

1 Who we are

Chopster is a desktop application developed by ARIS NANDA SAPUTRA ("we", "us", "the developer"). This policy applies to the Chopster application and to this website.

For the purposes of applicable data protection law, the developer named above is the party responsible for the application. However, because the application runs locally on your device and we receive no data from it, we do not hold any personal data about you.

2 What we do not collect

To be unambiguous, we do not collect, receive or have access to:

  • your TikTok password or login credentials;
  • the videos, captions or other content you process with the application;
  • your usage history, job records or log files, which remain on your device;
  • your IP address, device identifiers, or browsing behaviour;
  • any analytics, crash reports or telemetry.

3 Information the app processes

The application processes the following information locally in order to function:

Categories of data processed by the application, and their purpose.
CategoryExamplesPurpose
Platform authorisation data OAuth access token, refresh token, token expiry, account identifier issued by the platform To carry out the publishing action you explicitly authorised
Basic profile information Display name and account handle of the connected account Only so you can confirm which account is connected
Your content Video files, captions, titles, privacy settings that you choose To prepare and deliver that content on your instruction
Local application data Settings, job history, local log files and diagnostic screenshots So the application works correctly and so you can review what it did

The application does not request any profile data beyond what is needed to display the connected account. It requests no email address, no follower lists, no direct messages and no contact data.

4 Why we process it

The application processes the information above solely to perform the action you instructed it to perform — that is, to publish your own content to your own account — and to remember your settings between sessions.

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, the basis is your consent, given expressly when you authorise the connection between the application and your platform account, and you may withdraw that consent at any time.

5 Where it is stored

All of the information described above is stored locally on your own device, within your Windows user profile — typically under a folder such as %APPDATA%\ChopsterByAris.

We operate no server, no database and no cloud storage for this application. Consequently, no copy of your data exists on our side, and there is no transfer of your data to us.

6 Sharing and transfers

We do not sell, rent, trade or otherwise share your information with anyone. We do not share data with advertisers, data brokers or business partners, and we do not use your data to train any model.

The only outbound network communication the application performs is the direct connection between your device and the platform you authorised (for example, the official TikTok API endpoints). That connection exists only to carry out the publishing action you requested and is governed by that platform's own privacy policy.

7 Retention and deletion

Because data is stored locally, retention is entirely under your control:

  • Disconnecting an account inside the application immediately deletes the stored access token and associated session data for that account.
  • Revoking access from your TikTok account settings immediately prevents any further action by the application on that account.
  • Uninstalling the application and deleting its local data folder removes all remaining settings, history and logs from your device permanently.
  • Since we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to erase on our side.

8 Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and to data portability. Because we hold no personal data about you, these rights are effectively exercised directly by you on your own device.

You can exercise them at any time by inspecting, editing or deleting the application's local data folder, or by disconnecting the connected account. If you have any question about how to do this, you are welcome to contact us using the details in section 13.

9 Security

Your data is protected by the security of your own device and your Windows user account. We recommend that you use a device you trust, keep your operating system and browser updated, and protect your user account with a strong password.

All network communication with platform APIs is carried out over encrypted HTTPS connections. No credentials are ever transmitted to us.

10 Analytics and tracking

The application contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels, and no telemetry of any kind. This website contains no cookies and no third-party scripts; it is a set of static pages that do nothing but display text.

11 Children

The application is not intended for use by children under the minimum age required to hold a social media account in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly process data relating to such children.

12 Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version will always be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Material changes will be reflected in a new version number.

13 Contact

If you have any question about this Privacy Policy or about how your information is handled, please contact us at Arisnanda980@gmail.com.
